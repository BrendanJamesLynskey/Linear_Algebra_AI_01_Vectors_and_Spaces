# Vectors &amp; Vector Spaces

Deck 01 of the [Linear Algebra for AI / ML](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/LLM_Hub_Linear_Algebra) series.

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Linear_Algebra_AI_01_Vectors_and_Spaces/

The geometric foundations behind every embedding: vectors, span, basis, linear independence, dimension, coordinates, subspaces, and the strange behaviour of high-dimensional spaces that lets LLMs pack many more meaningful directions into a hidden state than they have neurons. Includes an interactive 2D vector playground.

## What's inside

- Three ways to look at a vector (arrow / tuple / abstract point)
- Vector operations and the linearity rule
- The eight axioms of a vector space and five examples that recur in ML
- Linear combinations and span &mdash; the geometry of "what can I reach?"
- Linear independence and the redundancy view
- Basis, dimension and coordinates
- Change of basis and why it matters for PCA, RoPE and interpretability
- Subspaces and the affine vs linear distinction
- Interactive 2D vector playground (drag heads, tune coefficients, watch span collapse)
- Counter-intuitive behaviour in $\mathbb{R}^{4096}$ &mdash; superposition and the Johnson-Lindenstrauss bound

Single-page HTML, KaTeX-rendered maths, no build step. Open `index.html` directly.
